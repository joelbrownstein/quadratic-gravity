## Consequential findings from this pass

1. **Running needs an observable definition.** The 2024 momentum-running proposal is followed by the 2025 gauge-dependence calculation and 2026 analysis of process-dependent IR logarithms. These do not undo Stelle's counterterm renormalizability result. They do block the unqualified inference that one preferred pair of beta functions establishes physical UV behavior. The scalar finite-volume coupling should be defined operationally, with perturbative scheme matching, before comparison.
2. **A massless scalar reduction is not automatically the high-energy limit of massive amplitudes.** Salvio–Strumia–Vitti section 4.5 explains a singular limit when asymptotic states coalesce. Anderson et al. prove perturbative statements for a specified massless Euclidean scalar setting. These are different objects; the manuscript must identify which one is sampled.
3. **The inflationary disagreement has a follow-up.** Liu–Afshordi retain the running Euler term in a revised one-loop RG-improved saddle. Their stated tensor lower envelope is about 0.008 rather than the original roughly 0.01. This is a scenario with strong matter/state/truncation assumptions, not an adjudicated resolution or a generic QQG bound.
4. **Dark-energy evidence must be dated.** DESI DR2 IV reports 2.7σ for its DESI+CMB combination and 3.2σ with its supernova addition, with a Lyα AP measurement compatible with ΛCDM. These are different combinations from the 2025 BAO headline. Updating a likelihood requires its joint covariance and exact release; it does not mean appending overlapping summaries.

## Missing links that define research tasks

- Classical conformal restriction -> parent quantum limit: integration measure, constraints, Jacobians, counterterms and contour.
- Scalar Euclidean continuum -> causal observables: physical states, continuation and composite-operator renormalization.
- Renormalized dimensionless coupling -> Newton's G or a cosmological scale: a calculated matching relation and scale setting. Dimensional transmutation alone does not identify a generated scale with an observed one.
- Flat conformal scalar -> black-hole center: curved reference geometry, Weyl-active dynamics, boundary/regularity conditions and stability.
- PS running -> dark energy: infrared action, matter and perturbation equations; a phenomenological f(R) control does not supply this link.

## Proposed completion criteria, not literature results

The near-term calculation is scalar lattice validation and step scaling. Its completion is independent of full tensor simulations. A later cosmological benchmark asks whether geometry and growth distinguish specified ΛCDM, evolving-dark-energy and viable f(R) controls on hidden simulations at a stated false-preference rate. Neural emulation is adopted only if its error, calibration and total amortized cost improve on the exact-solver baseline. No novel prediction is claimed until these calculations are performed.

- **Held–Zhang, Instability of spherically-symmetric black holes in Quadratic Gravity, arXiv:2209.01867 / DOI 10.1103/PhysRevD.107.064060 (2023).** Primary abstract/text inspected. Stability is an essential selection question; the two known branches have a long-wavelength instability below their critical intersection radius.

Record the authors' discussion of possible weakenings without asserting a settled admissibility result. Complex contours avoiding singularities are not automatically a proof of singularity resolution.
- **Tseytlin, Comments on 4-derivative scalar theory in 4 dimensions, arXiv:2212.10599v3 (2023).** Primary abstract checked: one-loop nonunitarity for the specified quartic-derivative sibling model and external states. Do not present it as a direct refutation of the PS construction.
