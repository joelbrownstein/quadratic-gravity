## Two research waves and their deliverables

**Observational wave:** define a model-to-data record; test expansion shape with the sound-horizon cancellation in the AP ratio; test growth jointly; assess scenario-specific tensor and extra-radiation predictions. No real-data fits have been run.

**Simulation wave:** define a periodic scalar regulator, zero-mode removal, action derivative, and finite-lattice integrability bound; validate algebraic identities; measure pilot autocorrelations and costs before production. The supplied validation script performs deterministic stencil checks only. It is not an HMC sampler or an interacting simulation.

The finite-lattice bound is an elementary derivation for the chosen nonnegative gradient stencil and telescoping Laplacian. It addresses the generic coercivity concern raised in the abstract-only review at fixed lattice size. It neither proves a continuum limit nor transfers automatically to other measures or counterterms.
