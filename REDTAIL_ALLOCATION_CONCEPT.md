# Redtail allocation: working concept

Prepared 21 November 2026. Proposed title: **Calibrated AI for distinguishing dark energy from modified gravity**.

The scientific deliverable is the geometry–growth recovery-power map in manuscript section 9. Compare adaptive emulator training with a space-filling baseline at matched calibration and frontier accuracy, retaining solver checks and counting total costs. Begin with summary-data mocks and linear-scale reference calculations. The independently useful PS scalar pilot precedes any optional learned-sampler comparison; no PS-to-cosmology matching is assumed.

The official [CHPC November launch announcement](https://www-prod.chpc.utah.edu/newsletter_updates/read.php?article=redtail-launch&month=11&year=2026) lists **1 December 2026** for Winter proposals, with awards starting **1 January 2027**. It describes project-based allocations and a 15,000 GPU-hour and/or 100,000 CPU-core-hour quarterly request limit. Those limits are not this project's budget.

A submission still needs measured pilot timings, requested resources, milestones, project/group setup and verification of the applicable eligibility and charging terms. No award, free entitlement, partner participation or hardware-specific speedup is asserted. This is a concept note, not a submitted or submission-ready application.
