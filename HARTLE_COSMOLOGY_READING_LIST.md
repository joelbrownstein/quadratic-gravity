## Primary reading order and evidence

1. **Hartle–Hawking, Wave function of the Universe (1983)**, [DOI 10.1103/PhysRevD.28.2960](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.28.2960). Foundational state proposal, already in the bibliography. Distinguish the state from the separate Hartle–Hawking thermal black-hole construction.
2. **Hartle–Hawking–Hertog, The Classical Universes of the No-Boundary Quantum State (2008)**, [arXiv:0803.1663](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0803.1663), [published paper](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.77.123537). Relevant primary text checked, especially sections II–III and the summary. The classicality condition is |∇I_R| much smaller than |∇S|, for a wave function with exp[(-I_R+iS)/hbar] and slowly varying prefactor. Their discussion of singularity resolution concerns continued predictivity for observables despite singular classical extrapolations; it does not prove metric completeness for every history.
3. **Hartle–Hertog, Arrows of Time in the Bouncing Universes of the No-boundary Quantum State (2012)**, [arXiv:1104.1733v3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1104.1733), [published paper](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.85.103524). Primary abstract and relevant text checked, including sections VI–IX. The fluctuation arrow is bidirectional away from the minimum in their bouncing histories, and can retain one direction through recollapse. The thermodynamic association is argued in that model; no universal reversal at a turnaround follows.
4. **Hartle, The Quantum Mechanical Arrows of Time (2013 preprint; 2014 chapter)**, [arXiv:1301.2844](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1301.2844), [chapter](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-88-470-5217-8_8). Primary abstract/text accessed and publisher metadata checked. Time-neutral fixed-background models allow local arrows consistent with global symmetry; entropy needs a coarse graining. The publisher identifies the book as Quantum Theory: A Two-Time Success Story (2014), rather than the provisional book reference in the old arXiv record.
5. **Hartle–Hertog, Quantum transitions between classical histories (2015)**, [arXiv:1502.06770v3](https://arxiv.org/html/1502.06770v3), [published paper](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.92.063509). Relevant primary text checked, especially section VI. Quantum transition probabilities connect patches of classical predictability. The black-hole application is a discussion, not a completed evaporation or singularity calculation.
6. **Kantowski–Sachs, Some Spatially Homogeneous Anisotropic Relativistic Cosmological Models (1966)**, [DOI 10.1063/1.1704952](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.1704952). Publisher issue metadata verified. The Schwarzschild-interior metric in the manuscript is obtained by direct coordinate substitution; this checks its homogeneous anisotropic form without pretending the original full text was read. The zero-spin geometry remains Lorentzian and has nonzero Weyl curvature.
7. **Feldbrugge–Lehners–Turok, No smooth beginning for spacetime (2017)**, [arXiv:1705.00192v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.00192), [paper](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.119.171301), alongside **Diaz Dorronsoro–Halliwell–Hartle–Hertog–Janssen, The Real No-Boundary Wave Function in Lorentzian Quantum Cosmology (2017)**, [arXiv:1705.05340v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.05340), [paper](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.96.043505). Primary abstracts checked. Different contour prescriptions produce different perturbation conclusions. These are model-specific arguments, not an adjudication of a complete QQG path integral.
8. **Liu–Quintin–Afshordi (2510.18733v2), Shinn (2609.26827), Liu–Afshordi (2609.24912v1)**. The original QQG paper's primary text, paragraphs around its no-boundary hemisphere, was checked directly; it explicitly calls the onset speculative and leaves instanton/path-integral work for later. The running-Euler follow-up changes the saddle calculation. These sources motivate a QQG state calculation, rather than establish normalized physical probabilities or an entropy arrow.

## What the proposed 3+1 -> 4 connection can mean

Both Lorentzian (three space, one time) and Euclidean descriptions have four dimensions. An analytic continuation or complex saddle is a prescription for an amplitude. A real continuous signature-changing metric would pass through degeneracy. Quantum histories supply a candidate predictive framework where a single classical spacetime fails; that is distinct from geometric regularity and from an arrow of time. The new cosmology subsections state those distinctions while preserving the physical research question.

## Later numerical questions

- On a fixed curved reference geometry and with explicit boundary conditions, what are the zero/negative modes and renormalized conformal correlations? A scalar computation is limited to that prescribed background and measure.
- For a fully specified QQG state, which saddle branches satisfy the constraints and a derived classicality criterion, and what perturbation covariance follows on their Lorentzian branches?
- In a Weyl-active interior ansatz, do anisotropy, junction conditions and stability permit the proposed continuation? These require more than the flat PS scalar.

No arrow is inferred from Monte Carlo evolution. Entropy claims need a physical coarse graining and a measured or calculated observable. None of these later calculations has been run.
